I designed this student attendance concept to address proxy check-ins and slow administrative recap in campus attendance workflows. The core objective was to make attendance verification more trustworthy while keeping the student experience simple enough for daily use.

As UI/UX Designer and Developer, I handled user flow design, location-verification logic, and prototype structure. The guiding hypothesis was that a geofence-based check-in flow with clear status feedback could improve attendance integrity without adding unnecessary friction for students and faculty.

I mapped user pain points, defined required data states, and tested key scenarios such as valid location, invalid location, and unstable connection behavior. The biggest tradeoff was balancing strict verification with practical usability, so the design prioritized clarity and reliability first. The project established a strong direction for campus digital attendance and gave me deeper experience in combining UX decisions with location-sensitive system constraints.
