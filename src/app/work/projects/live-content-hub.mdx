Live Content Digital Hub was built to replace manual internal media distribution with a centralized and scheduled system. The main goal was to make content updates faster, more consistent, and easier to control across organizational endpoints.

I worked as Full Stack Developer, delivering the CMS workflow, role-based access handling, and auto-play viewer behavior. The working assumption was that centralizing upload and scheduling in one operational flow would reduce version mismatch and improve execution efficiency for non-technical teams.

I analyzed stakeholder needs, simplified publishing steps, and validated upload, scheduling, and playback continuity under routine operational scenarios. Timing consistency and endpoint stability were the hardest parts, so release priorities focused on dependable behavior over advanced analytics. The result is a maintainable platform foundation that can evolve with approval workflows, richer monitoring, and stronger operational reporting.
